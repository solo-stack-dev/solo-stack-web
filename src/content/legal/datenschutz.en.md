## 3. General information

### Legal bases

We process personal data under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where necessary for a contract with you or steps taken at your request before entering into a contract. Legal obligations may require processing under Article 6(1)(c) GDPR. We rely on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for the legitimate interests specified in this notice. Where we obtain consent, Article 6(1)(a) GDPR is the legal basis; you can withdraw it at any time with future effect.

Express consent to processing special categories of personal data is governed by Article 9(2)(a) GDPR. Such information is not required to use the website or contact form. Express consent to an exceptional transfer to a third country may be governed by Article 49(1)(a) GDPR; activating a video does not constitute blanket consent to transfers without safeguards.

Storing information on your device and accessing it are also subject to section 25 of the German Telecommunications and Digital Services Data Protection Act (TDDDG). Consent is required under subsection 1 unless an exception under subsection 2 applies, particularly where the operation is strictly necessary to provide a service you explicitly request. This assessment is separate from the legal basis for subsequent processing of personal data.

### Recipients and international transfers

Service providers receive data where necessary for the purposes described, as do public authorities or other recipients where a legal obligation or another legal basis applies. Providers processing data on our behalf are engaged under an agreement pursuant to Article 28 GDPR. Platform operators may be independent controllers for their own processing; joint controllership is governed by Article 26 GDPR.

International providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Transfers rely on an adequacy decision under Article 45 GDPR, such as for US recipients with relevant active certification under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, or appropriate safeguards under Article 46 GDPR, particularly European Commission standard contractual clauses. Certification applies only within its respective scope. You can obtain information about safeguards from us and through the linked provider notices.

### Retention

We retain personal data for as long as necessary for the relevant purpose. Once that purpose ends, we delete them unless legal retention duties or other permissible grounds require continued retention, such as establishing, exercising or defending legal claims. Data are deleted once those grounds cease to apply. Valid erasure requests and withdrawals of consent are considered subject to these conditions. Data processed by platform providers as independent controllers are subject to their retention rules and deletion options.

### Security

The website uses HTTPS with TLS encryption to protect transmission. Nevertheless, internet communications, particularly email, may have security vulnerabilities. Complete protection against third party access cannot be guaranteed.

## 6. Cookies and browser storage

In addition to cookies, local storage and session storage can store information in your browser. Local storage generally remains until you delete the data or your browser removes them. Session storage is limited to the respective browser session or tab.

The website stores your chosen light or dark appearance and local game high scores in local storage. Session storage holds the state of small hidden website features, for example to prevent a console message from appearing on every page load or to retain a discovered appearance during the session. These functions do not transmit these local values to us as a game account or usage profile.

Processing serves your chosen appearance and interactive features. Where personal data are involved, our legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR is providing these features in a user friendly way. Storage access strictly necessary to provide an explicitly requested feature may fall under section 25(2)(2) TDDDG; other access requires consent under section 25(1) TDDDG. Third party providers may use their own storage technologies after you activate their services, particularly YouTube.

You can delete or block cookies and website storage in your browser. This may remove settings and high scores or restrict functionality.

## 11. Your rights

### Access, rectification and erasure

Subject to the statutory conditions, you have a right of access under Article 15 GDPR, particularly to the processed data, their source, recipients, purposes and retention period. Under Article 16 GDPR, you may request correction of inaccurate data and completion of incomplete data, and under Article 17 GDPR their erasure. Exercising your rights is generally free of charge.

### Restriction of processing

Under Article 18 GDPR, you may request restriction if you contest the accuracy of data while it is checked; if processing is unlawful and you request restriction instead of erasure; if we no longer need the data but you need them for legal claims; or if the balancing of interests following an objection under Article 21(1) GDPR is still pending. Apart from storage, restricted data may generally be processed only with consent, for legal claims, to protect another person's rights or for important public interests of the EU or a Member State.

### Portability and withdrawal of consent

For data you have provided that we process automatically on the basis of your consent or a contract, Article 20 GDPR entitles you to receive them in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format. Direct transmission to another controller is available where technically feasible. You can withdraw consent at any time with future effect. This does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

### Objection under Article 21 GDPR

**Where processing relies on Article 6(1)(e) or (f) GDPR, you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. This also applies to profiling based on those provisions. We will then stop processing the affected data unless we demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds overriding your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing serves to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.**

**You can object to processing for direct marketing at any time without giving a particular reason. This also applies to related profiling. The data concerned will then no longer be processed for direct marketing.**

### Complaints to a supervisory authority

Under Article 77 GDPR, you can complain to a data protection supervisory authority, particularly in the place of your habitual residence, workplace or the suspected infringement. Other administrative or judicial remedies remain unaffected. The [Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision](https://www.lda.bayern.de/) is responsible for private sector organisations in Bavaria.
