## What the owner wants
A personal brand homepage for the German YouTube channel "Solo Stack" (a developer who builds his own apps with AI). Unique, flashy, a bit over the top, craft-level, never generic. Calm and premium at its core, cozier than a business podcast, but the homepage is a showpiece. Every section is its own crafted scene, and together they flow as one piece.

## The concept: "one night in the studio"
Scrolling the homepage = one night passing. Each section is an hour with a tiny clock label: 20:00 moonrise hero, 21:00 "what I make", 22:00 game, 01:00 night shift (coding at night), 02:00 night radio (music + socials), 04:00 latest video (open-air cinema), 06:00 sunrise footer where the sky melts back into butter-yellow pigment paper (the left side of the banner), so the page ends where the brand starts.

## Art directions
- A "Painted night" (BASE): everything looks hand painted in watercolor like the banner. Pigment blooms, granulation, soft bleeding edges, ink linework that draws itself, real paper grain. Cozy and premium, a touch of Studio Ghibli calm (original style, no imitation of specific frames).
- B "Paper-cut lightbox" (for depth, especially the hero): scenes built from 6 to 8 layers of cut watercolor paper, lit from behind by the moon, real depth, soft cast shadows between layers, tiny floating dust in moonlight. Built later with Threlte (Three.js for Svelte), so it must look achievable as layered planes.
- C "Ink and manga" (accent only): black ink, screentone, speed lines, yellow as the only color. Used for short moments and easter eggs.

## Palette and type
Butter yellow (warm, like the moon and the paper), anthracite night (not pure black, slightly warm), warm off-white paper, a hint of deeper ochre in pigment blooms. Type: heavy geometric sans for headings (like the SOLO STACK wordmark; Geist family on the site), small monospace labels for the clock times. Yellow is light, never neon.

## Mascot
A drawn/chibi version of the owner (short dark hair, stubble, black tee; at night maybe a dark hoodie), painted in watercolor with ink outlines, friendly and calm. Keep it consistent with the profile photo's features.

## Rules
- Original artwork only. Anime/game references are subtle nods, never copied characters, logos, or scenes (no Nintendo Triforce logo, no real Pokémon, etc.; allusions through shape, color, situation).
- Text inside mockups is placeholder: keep it minimal and short (German), it will be replaced by text from another model later. The wordmark SOLO STACK and clock labels like "20:00" are fine. No invented stats.
- Mockups should look like real website screens (desktop 16:9 or tall page crops, plus at least one mobile 9:19.5 where it matters), with believable UI: nav, small labels, buttons, but the art dominates.
- Quality bar: if a result looks like generic AI stock art, a template, or a Dribbble clone, regenerate. Check every image yourself before keeping it; fix anatomy, text errors, and brand drift. Prefer fewer excellent images over many weak ones.
- Write ONLY inside your assigned output folder (below). Other agents work in parallel in sibling folders; another agent sets up the code project. Do not install packages or touch any other files.
- In your folder, save: the final PNG/JPG images with descriptive file names, PROMPTS.md (exact prompts and reference images used per image), and README.md (per image: what it shows, the interaction/animation idea in 2-3 sentences, how it would be built: SVG/CSS/Threlte, and rough cost). Keep rejected attempts out (or in a `rejects/` subfolder, max a few).
