## Normal seal: exact generation prompt

```text
Use case: product-mockup.
Asset type: final production transparent PNG wax seal for solo-stack.dev contact button; highest available visual quality, native alpha transparency.
Create ONE real poured sealing-wax blob viewed exactly straight down, square canvas ideally 800 by 800. This is handmade wax, not a medal. Matte butter-yellow to ochre pigmented wax (#fcd772 to #e2a93b). Dry satin-free wax surface, subtle fine wax grain and tiny organic imperfections, restrained diffuse material shading. A visibly irregular asymmetrical puddle silhouette: uneven pressed outer rim with a few broad natural lobes, thin locally squashed skirt, one small connected drip/spill at lower right. Not a perfect circle, not a scalloped regular badge. Rim is low and partly interrupted, NOT a thick rounded donut. Broad shallow central pressed field with ONE simple embossed crescent moon impression, opening toward upper right, large enough to read at 56–72 CSS pixels. Single clean crescent; no stars or lettering. Keep moon relief softly defined in slightly deeper ochre so its shape remains unmistakable at 64 pixels.
Composition: isolated seal, center of stamped field exactly at center of canvas; wax including spill occupies about 84% of canvas width and height, generous clear transparent margins, no cropping, no perspective, no rotation of camera. No background surface. Actual native transparency, never white or checkerboard baked into RGB. At most a tiny very soft neutral ink contact shadow immediately beneath wax. No floating drop shadow, no glow.
Lighting: broad ambient flat diffuse light, NO glossy/specular highlights, NO white glints, NO metallic gold, NO smooth gradient across face, NO polished bevel, NO coin or emoji appearance, NO plastic. Warm but softly desaturated butter wax rather than saturated orange gold. A convincing handmade material silhouette is more important than ornate detail. Final asset must remain readable on both anthracite #18191b and warm paper #fdf9ee. No text, no watermark. Produce only the single normal/unpressed seal.
```

## Pressed seal: exact edit prompt

Input: the normal seal generation, supplied as the single reference/edit target. `transparent_background: true`.

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit.
Edit target: the supplied transparent normal wax seal. Create the PRESSED STATE of this exact same seal for an in-place UI image swap. Highest available visual quality; keep native alpha transparency.
CRITICAL invariants: keep identical square canvas dimensions, camera, position, center, scale, crescent shape and orientation, exact irregular outer silhouette including every lobe and the small connected drip at lower right. Keep wax perimeter pixels and alpha boundary exactly in place. Do not recenter or enlarge. Do not invent a different blob. Keep all characteristic surface grain and pores aligned wherever possible.
Change ONLY the interior wax relief: the same wax has been pressed slightly flatter, the crescent impression is slightly deeper and more clearly defined with muted ochre inner recess shading, and the overall wax is a touch darker (about 5–7 percent). The crescent stays in exactly the same position and same footprint. Outer skirt remains in place so swapping images never jumps. Flatten the low rim's relief very subtly. Preserve matte butter-to-ochre pigmented wax (#fcd772 to #e2a93b); no glossy or specular highlights, no metallic gold look, no white glints, no polished bevels, no glow, no smooth all-over gradient. Keep real dry wax texture; not clay, cheese, coin, plastic, or emoji.
Transparent background stays transparent. No added backdrop or cast shadow. No text, no watermark. Output one pressed seal image only.
```

## Export and verification

- Art was generated by image_gen. Python/Pillow only performs the requested deterministic sizing, alpha registration, background previews, and measurements; it does not draw the seal.
- The edit retained the same composition but its native alpha perimeter differed by approximately 2 source pixels. Both exports use the intersection (minimum) of the two native alpha channels, removing that small drift. No background was synthesized or keyed out.
- Removed only native alpha values 1–3 to suppress invisible detached generation specks. Retained native fractional coverage elsewhere, including the almost-opaque interior at alpha 252–253.
- Shared crop `(34, 59, 1178, 1197)`; shared resize to 672×668; shared placement `(64, 66)` on an 800×800 transparent canvas. Identical alpha channels, bounds, and center in both final PNGs.
- Premultiplied-alpha Lanczos resizing; fully transparent RGB set to zero; explicit sRGB PNG metadata.
- Previews at 56, 64, 72, and 200 pixels on `#18191b` and `#fdf9ee`. `qa-seals.png` presents the requested 64px and 200px comparisons. `qa-alpha-edges.png` magnifies 64px pixels and full-resolution edge crops.
- Visually checked both proof sheets: crescent and drip remain legible at 64px; normal/pressed states retain their silhouette; no visible pale, white, or dark fringe on either background. Numeric report records a few alpha-1 neutral RGB resampling pixels (less than 0.4% coverage), not a visible halo.
- `previews/alpha-report.json` contains dimensions, byte sizes, SHA-256 hashes, alpha statistics, transparent border checks, and exact alpha equality. `previews/export_and_check.py` reproduces outputs from the retained originals.

No visitor-facing text was created. No application code or existing art was changed. The pressed image expresses the deeper relief and darker wax; any 2px button-depress translation is left to the consuming UI and is not baked into these aligned assets.
